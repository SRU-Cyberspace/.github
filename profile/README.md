## About

Cyberspace Club is the student cybersecurity organization at Slippery Rock University. We give Slippery Rock students a place to explore cybersecurity, practice defensive and offensive skills ethically, and grow into professionals who look out for each other. We run workshops, CTF practice, speaker nights, and hands-on labs for majors and curious beginners alike.

## What we do

- **Workshop nights.** Hands-on sessions on networking, Linux, web security, and defensive tooling. Bring a laptop.
- **CTF practice.** Team practice for picoCTF, HackTheBox, and regional competitions. Beginners pair with veterans.
- **Career and community.** Resume reviews, speaker nights, and connections to SRU faculty and regional security meetups.
